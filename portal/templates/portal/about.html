Accessibility & Inclusive Design

High-Contrast Palette

Dark midnight navy (#09182D) and royal crest blue (#0B3970) paired with crisp white surfaces exceed WCAG AA contrast ratios for comfortable reading.

Keyboard-First Navigation

Includes a hidden “Skip to main content” link, semantic HTML landmarks, and high-visibility gold focus rings (:focus-visible) for keyboard-only users.

Descriptive Media & Responsive Layout

All institutional emblems include descriptive alternative text, and CSS Grid/Flexbox adapts smoothly from desktop down to mobile viewports.
